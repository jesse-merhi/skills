# Reader reset

1. Find the missing premise.

   Identify the smallest piece of context the reader needs before the idea can
   make sense.

   Done when that premise fits in one or two sentences without relying on the
   preceding explanation.

2. Rewrite for this reader.

   Lead with the idea in everyday language and introduce its technical name
   only after the idea is clear. Add a concrete example when it helps. Match
   technical density to the reader's
   demonstrated expertise. Prefer established terms from the applicable
   `CONTEXT.md` and briefly define unfamiliar terms. When the repo has more than
   one context, follow `CONTEXT-MAP.md` to the file for the current area.

   Done when the reader can understand or act without recovering context from
   the failed explanation, while the important nuance remains.
