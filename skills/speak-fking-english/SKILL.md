# Speak Fking English

Run this as the last editing pass before returning a final response.

## Final Response Pass

1. Load `wait-what`. Apply its reader reset to the draft: restore the missing
   premise, lead with the outcome in everyday language, match the reader's
   technical level, and use a concrete example only when it helps.

2. Imagine teaching the same content to this person. Decide what supporting
   material they would actually use to understand or act. A focused code block,
   command and output, table, screenshot, recording, or diagram may help; plain
   prose is often enough.

3. Load `show-me` to make the visual decision. Add a visual only when the
   subject itself is visual or has a relationship, sequence, state change,
   spatial layout, or comparison that prose makes the reader reconstruct.

4. Prefer the smallest useful support. Keep one fact, a short status, a simple
   outcome, and an easy list in prose. Do not create HTML, a diagram, a table, or
   a screenshot merely to look polished or prove that this checkpoint ran.

5. Remove repeated explanation, unnecessary headings, jargon, and any visual
   that does not make a specific idea easier to learn. Return the revised answer
   as the whole final response.

## Done Means

- The outcome comes first and the missing premise is present.
- The reader can understand the answer without the working-session context.
- Technical detail matches what the reader needs to understand or act.
- Supporting material has a clear teaching job; otherwise it is absent.
- Evidence shows the real changed behavior and is not replaced by an explainer.
