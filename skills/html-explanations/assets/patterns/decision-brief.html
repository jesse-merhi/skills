Decision brief · 31 July 2026

Retry later, without hiding failures.

Use bounded exponential backoff for transient delivery failures, then move exhausted jobs to the existing dead-letter queue. It gives the queue time to recover without retrying forever.

Recommendation

Choose option B: 5 attempts over about 15 minutes, with jitter and a visible terminal state.

The decision turns on three constraints

5 minordinary provider recovery window
< 1%acceptable duplicate-delivery risk
1 ownermust be able to inspect every exhausted job

Cost we accept

A transient failure may now take 15 minutes to become terminal. The job UI must show attempt count and next retry time so this delay is visible.
