# Improving the rating

The overall rating is the weaker applicable side: proof quality or patch quality. More media won't improve the result when proof is already sufficient or not applicable.

To improve confidence, follow the latest review's concrete rank-up advice: resolve findings, keep the diff focused, prove the changed behavior on the current head, and settle relevant compatibility or owner decisions. Use real runtime evidence for runtime claims.

For a correct, security-clear patch without findings, reviewer confidence of 0.80 reaches diamond; 0.95 reaches challenger. A P3 finding caps the patch at platinum, P2 at gold, and P0/P1 at silver or worse. The bot supplies confidence; the author doesn't choose it.

Missing proof scores poorly. Sufficient terminal/log evidence can reach diamond; appropriate screenshots, recordings, or linked artifacts can score higher. Choose what proves the claim rather than adding decorative evidence.
