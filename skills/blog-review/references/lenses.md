# Review Lenses

## Story And Structure

Trace the article's promise, setup, escalation, turning points, consequences,
and ending. Report missing causal links, duplicated beats, sections arriving
before their setup, stakes that disappear, examples without a job, and endings
that do not resolve the opening. Judge the whole story before individual lines.

## Paragraph And Sentence Flow

Read aloud mentally. Track referents, clause attachment, information order,
sentence-shape repetition, abrupt paragraph openings, redundant reversals,
parenthetical interruption, and transitions that announce movement without
performing it. A long sentence is not a defect by itself; a short sentence is
not automatically punchy.

## Voice And Authenticity

Compare against canonical writing with the same rhetorical role. Look for
generic AI cadence, invented audience objections, abstract throat-clearing,
unearned drama, false certainty, personality being sanded away, comedy added
where Jesse would be direct, and superficial copying of punctuation without the
underlying thought pattern. Cite corpus evidence for voice claims.

## Reader And Factual Continuity

Act as an intelligent reader without project history. Find unexplained terms,
missing context, ambiguous chronology, unsupported conclusions, contradictory
claims, examples that do not prove the point, accidental changes of meaning,
and factual statements that need verification. Separate research needs from
prose findings.

## Holistic Cold Pass

After revisions, review the full article without prior findings. Ask only
whether a real reader problem remains. Do not re-litigate accepted stylistic
choices unless the final text creates new evidence.
